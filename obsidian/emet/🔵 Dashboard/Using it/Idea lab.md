# Idea lab

Status: ✅ Live · part of [[emet HQ (dashboard)]]

Where **what emet sells** gets decided. Migration 0014.
- Anyone pitches an idea → AI (member's own free Groq key) **researches it on the web** with sources
- Scores **7 criteria** against our goal: demand, ≥50% margin, €0 start, time to first sale, reach €30K, team fit, risk
- App computes the **score /100 + verdict**, the numbers, a **7-day €0 test** (→ tasks in one tap) and stronger versions
- Team **votes and discusses**
- Deep dive → [[Idea lab council]] · first clients → [[Scout (lead finder)]]
