# 🔵 How the dashboard works

> **Bubble 2 of 3: emet HQ, the app where the team does its daily work.** Live at [emet-hq.vercel.app](https://emet-hq.vercel.app), invite-only. The full overview is [[emet HQ (dashboard)]]. The other bubbles are [[🟠 About emet]] (who we are) and [[🟢 My projects]] (what we build). Home: [[🏔️ emet Dashboard]]

## 1. You log in → your day
- [[Today]]: your first screen, with tasks, calendar (Week/Month) and the daily pop-up
- [[Google Calendar]]: your Google events appear in Today
- [[Phone version]]: the same dashboard on your phone

## 2. You do your work
- [[Tasks page]]: one page for every task (All / My Tasks / EMET / Projects)
- [[Office check-in]]: phones check people in/out at the office, hours counted; leaders send team notifications
- [[Task boards]] · [[Team tasks]]: older boards that fold into the Tasks page
- [[My Projects]]: each project with its people, tasks, money, links, files and customers
- [[Documents]]: company documents + your private folder
- [[Base camp]]: your private area + the note-taker

## 3. Your group's section
- [[Finance tab]]: money in/out and Subscriptions
- [[Marketing section]]: includes the Prediction agent (MiroFish)
- Customer · Communications · Team Leaders (strikes, giving tasks, training leaders)

## 4. Finding what to sell and who to sell to
- [[Idea lab]] → [[Idea lab council]]: test ideas
- [[Scout (lead finder)]]: find customers for a strong idea

## 5. Working together
- [[Meeting log]]: meeting notes, kept 30 days in Drive
- [[Workspace tools]]: notifications, ⌘K, Ask emet, Friday report
- [[Notion]] · [[My connections & AI keys]]
- [[Overview (company dashboard)]]: the whole company at a glance
