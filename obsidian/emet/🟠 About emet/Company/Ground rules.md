# Ground rules

1. **€0 budget** — free plans, free tiers, free APIs only. If something costs money: stop and ask the team (decision logged in [[Decisions log]]).
2. **Languages** — docs and team talk in **English or Spanish**. Only emet HQ supports all **7**: English, Spanish, German, French, Finnish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian.
3. **The repo is the source of truth** — every change also updates `README.md` + `CHANGELOG.md`.
4. **Pull before editing**, never overwrite other people's work (15 people + their Claudes share it).
5. **Show the full diff and wait for "push it"** before any push.
6. **Team profiles are preliminary** — some members dispute their review results; never treat them as final roles.
