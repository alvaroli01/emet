# Weekly rhythm

| When | What | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Daily | Check-ins with each group | Operations monitor |
| Within 24 h of any sale/expense | Log it (Bookkeeper tool in [[Finance tab]]) | Bookkeeper |
| Weekly | Leaders' meeting (record it in [[Meeting log]], leaders-only) | Team leader |
| Weekly | Morale check (anonymous average for all) | Documentation & wellness |
| Friday | Profit report (auto-generated, [[Workspace tools]]) | Financial leader |
| Friday | Scorecard — KPIs vs targets | Project manager |
