| 🎋 **Bamboo weaving** (竹编) | **€3** | Woven bamboo ornaments (butterfly, gourd with tassel), small baskets/coasters | Slide calls it a "traditional handcraft *experience*" → could also be a workshop |
| 🪭 **Lacquered (marbled) bamboo fan** (漆扇) | **€5** | Pigments floated on water, the fan is dipped → every fan unique | **Best live-workshop idea**: people make their own at a stand (higher price, e.g. €8–10). Off-season (November) as a fan, so sell it as a souvenir |
| 👛 **Embroidered wallet** (钱包) | **€5–8** | Small hand-embroidered coin purse | Price spread → ask which models cost what |
| 🌿 **Herbal sachet** (香囊) | **€3** | Embroidered pouch with mugwort, mint and other herbs; for good luck / repels mosquitoes | Dried herbs: ask the exact contents (plant-import rules) |

**Quick read:** the prices look like **sale prices to visitors**, not what they cost. Everything is cheap (€2–8), so the money is in **volume**, a **bundle** (e.g. bookmark + sachet + bamboo charm = €7 instead of €8) and **workshops** (fan marbling, bamboo weaving), not in mark-up. Our share per item still needs agreeing.

## 🥠 Fortune cookies idea
- Two versions: Spanish message, and Chinese message + key card
- Chinese has no alphabet, so the "key" works best as: the message in characters + **pinyin** + a small numbered glossary card (or a QR to the translation). The Chinese students can write and check the messages
- Messages can carry raffle prizes ("show this cookie at the stand for a free …") → drives traffic to sponsors' stands
- To check: custom-printed cookies vs. buying plain ones and inserting our own slips; unit cost; food labelling rules for selling them #open

## 🤝 Sponsors — where to find them and how to win them
**Where to look**
1. **Chinese businesses in Bilbao/Bizkaia** — restaurants, supermarkets, bubble-tea shops, import shops. Walk in with the Chinese students (speaking Mandarin helps a lot). Ask for a raffle prize or €50–200
2. **Chinese community associations** in Bizkaia/Basque Country and Chinese chambers of commerce / business associations in Spain #open (find names)
3. **Chinese companies with Spanish offices** — phones/tech (Xiaomi, Huawei, Lenovo…), cars (BYD and other Chinese brands with Bilbao dealers), apps/e-commerce, banks in Madrid, airlines. Local dealers and shops are easier than head offices
4. **Official/culture side** — the Chinese Embassy's education & culture office, Confucius Institutes in Spain, the Chinese university itself
5. **Basque side with China links** — Basque companies that export to China, Bilbao city council culture agenda, Mondragon University (our own school)
6. Use [[Scout (lead finder)]] to list Chinese businesses in Bilbao (€0)

**How to make them want us** — a one-page sponsor pack (Spanish + English, ideally Chinese too):
- Who comes: Basque/Spanish public + international students + the Chinese delegation
- What they get: logo on posters/raffle tickets/socials, a stand or table, mention on stage, their product as a raffle prize, photos and a thank-you post, a "first China–Basque student culture fair" story
- Tiers (example): **Partner** in kind (prize or product) · **Silver** €200 · **Gold** €500 (+ stand + name in event title)
- In-kind is the easiest "yes" — ask for prizes first, cash second
