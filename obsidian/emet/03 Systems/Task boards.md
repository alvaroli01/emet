# Task boards

Status: ✅ Live · part of [[emet HQ (dashboard)]]

**6 boards:** 1 general company board + 1 per group. Built in (no Trello needed).
- Drag and drop, assignees, due dates, labels, checklists, comments
- Cards become projects with small tasks (person + date) and a progress bar
- **My Tasks** page; templates and **Repeat** on any card ([[Workspace tools]])
- Optional **Trello sync** per board, off by default (`dashboard/TRELLO_SETUP.md`)
- Inside each team section the board is one of the Tools (right column)
