# Team tasks

Status: ✅ Live · part of [[emet HQ (dashboard)]]

Added 15 Oct (migration 0024, 9 new permission tests).
- Team leaders/admins get **Assign tasks** (Team Leader section + shortcut on My Tasks)
- Give a task to everyone, a whole team, several people or themselves; optional details + due date
- People see it in **My Tasks → "From the team leader"** with a tick box
- Everyone sees who has finished (green tick) and who hasn't
- Only leaders create/delete; each person ticks only their own; notification to everyone who has logged in
